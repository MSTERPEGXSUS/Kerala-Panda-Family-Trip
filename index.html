The Itinerary

Click on each day to explore the plans and activities.

Arrive at Cochin International Airport at 1:45 PM. Meet the representative and begin the scenic journey towards Munnar.

En route visits:

  • Cheeyappara Waterfalls
  • Valara Waterfalls

Arrive in Munnar, check into the hotel and relax.

Overnight stay: Grand Cliff – Kohinoor Manor

After breakfast, enjoy a full day exploring the beautiful hill station of Munnar.

  • Mattupetty Dam
  • Echo Point
  • Kundala Lake
  • Tea plantations
  • Tea Factory
  • Eravikulam National Park (subject to availability)

Return to the hotel and relax.

Overnight stay: Grand Cliff – Kohinoor Manor

After breakfast, travel to Alleppey, famous for its beautiful backwaters.

Board the traditional Kerala houseboat around noon and enjoy a relaxing cruise surrounded by coconut palms, paddy fields and village life.

Meals included onboard:

  • Lunch
  • Evening tea and snacks
  • Dinner
  • Breakfast
Overnight stay: Blue Jelly – Super Luxury Houseboat with Upper Deck
4 bedrooms + 3 extra beds + 1 without bed

After breakfast, check out from the houseboat. One guest will travel separately to Cochin Airport, while the remaining guests continue towards Varkala.

Explore:

  • Papanasam Beach
  • Varkala Cliff

Check into the hotel and relax.

Overnight stay: Gateway Varkala – IHCL SeleQtions
Superior Room

After breakfast, check out and proceed towards Kovalam.

En route sightseeing:

  • Sree Padmanabhaswamy Temple
  • Puthen Maliga Palace Museum
  • Napier Museum
  • Poovar Boating

Continue to Kovalam, check in and enjoy some relaxing beach time.

Overnight stay: The Leela Kovalam – Sea View Villa

After breakfast, check out from the hotel.

Depending on the flight schedule, enjoy some free time for shopping or relaxation before travelling to Trivandrum International Airport.

End of a wonderful Kerala family holiday!
